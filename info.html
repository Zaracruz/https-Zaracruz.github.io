Echoes

How to Use Echoes

Home

The Home page is where the main experience happens. Here you can upload or select live listening to detect emotions within your songs, and see your the visualiser react in real time.

Settings

In Settings, you can customise how your visuals look. Change the style of the visual display and switch between different themes to match your vibe.

Emotion Control

Not everyone feels the same emotions from music — and that’s what this feature is for.

You can choose between:

  • Automatic Emotion Detection – the app decides the emotion based on the music
  • Manual Selection – you choose the emotion yourself

When you select an emotion manually, the visuals will display a consistent mood based on your choice.

This is especially useful if the app is connected to lighting systems, allowing you to control the atmosphere and set the exact mood you want.
